technohands

Stand anywhere, raise a hand. Instruments stay fixed to hands: the first pair of hands on screen plays the first pair of voices below, the next pair the next, and so on left to right. A closed fist means silence. Press cycle pairs to deal the eight voices into new pairs. That also happens by itself every three minutes, and waits until no hands are up. One 132bpm clock, so nothing drifts out of time.
